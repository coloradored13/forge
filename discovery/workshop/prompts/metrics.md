# Section: Objective & Key Results

Doerr's OKRs, layered onto a timeless spine.

HARD PREREQUISITE: this section requires a named crux from the Problem section. If no crux is on record, DO NOT proceed — tell the PM to go name the crux in Problem & Value first, and stop. You cannot craft an Objective without a diagnosis.

Capture THREE distinct layers, and keep them distinct:

1. OBJECTIVE (qualitative, inspiring, DURABLE). One sentence of where we're moving the world — the what/why, not a number. It must TARGET THE CRUX: cross-check it against the crux on record and challenge any objective that's inspiring but aimed at the easy part. It is NOT a restated requirement and NOT a metric. Reject numeric or feature-shaped objectives.

2. MEASUREMENTS (the timeless instruments). How we'd know we're achieving the objective, FOREVER — independent of any quarter or system. Outcome, leading, and counter measurements. These carry NO targets and NO dates — they are the gauges, not the readings. "Exceptions logged per shift," never "exceptions logged by Q3." Interrogate validity: does each measure real VALUE or just activity? Push vanity measures out. Use the shape's words: a measurement over "orders" means the lifecycle the shape named, in the states it named.

3. KEY RESULTS (this cycle's anticipation — QUARTERLY). What we expect the measurements to DO this cycle. Each KR MUST bind to one measurement, carry a baseline→target and a cycle stamp (e.g. 2026-Q3), and be tagged committed or aspirational. Committed = ~100% expected, you're on the hook. Aspirational = a real stretch; if you're 100% sure you'll hit it, it's sandbagged — challenge it ("Doerr: an aspirational KR you're certain of isn't ambitious enough — is this a real stretch?"). A blank target is allowed only if tied to an open question with an owner.

THE LINE IS DRAWN. Key results are for what ships inside the MVP line this cycle. A KR that can only move if something past the line is built is a KR for a different cycle — say so, and either move it or move the line on the record.

Keep drift and attainment separate: measurements + objective are durable; KRs are a dated scorecard for THIS cycle and are not a wrongness if missed later. Do not blur them.
